Social Media strategy for Instagram

Content pillars
📅
Event spotlight
Promote upcoming events — book clubs, movie nights, surfer meetups. Post 5–7 days before. Drive sign-ups with a friendly CTA.

📖
Book of the week
One personal pick or in-store favourite. Keep it opinionated — say why you love it. Speak to the Tarifa crowd: surfers, nomads, local regulars.

🌊
Tarifa life & vibe
The wind, the sea, the community. A shelf shot, a rainy reading afternoon, a cheeky surf-day caption. This is your brand glue.

Posting rhythm
Tuesday — Book pick. Mid-week scrollers are in reading mode. Personal, warm, conversational.

Thursday — Event post. Give the weekend crowd 2–3 days notice. Include date, time, vibe.

Saturday (optional 3rd post) — Lifestyle/vibe. The store is alive on weekends — share that energy.

Strategy lays out the three content pillars — event spotlights, book picks, and Tarifa life/vibe posts — plus the recommended posting rhythm of Tuesday, Thursday, and optionally Saturday.
Week 1 & Week 2 posts give you fully written captions, ready to copy, with photo tips for each one. The posts are built around your actual upcoming events and books already in the shop.
Caption toolkit is a library of reusable openers, CTAs, and hashtag sets you can mix and match for any future post.
A few things to note about the approach:
The tone is deliberately loose and community-first — not promotional. Things like "we don't gatekeep" and "no sign-up needed, just come" speak directly to the nomad and surfer crowd who are wary of anything that feels pushy. The CTAs are always present but light-touch.
Each book post ties back to something Tarifa-specific — the levante, the Strait, the lifestyle — so the recommendations feel personal rather than generic.

Book posts — stripped back, let the book speak, minimal hashtags
Event posts — direct, key details only, no overselling
Saturday vibe — short, dry, "We're open. 📚" as the closer
Tone across all — straightforward, no flourishes, vaguely Scandinavian
